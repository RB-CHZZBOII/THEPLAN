Morning Routine (6:00 - 6:30 AM)

This routine is designed to enhance productivity and set a positive tone for your day.

  • 6:00 AM - Wake Up & Hydrate: Avoid hitting snooze. Get out of bed immediately. Drink a large glass of water (250-500ml) to rehydrate your body after sleep. Consider adding a squeeze of lemon for an extra boost.
  • 6:05 AM - Light Stretching/Movement: Engage in 5-10 minutes of gentle stretching, yoga, or light calisthenics. This helps to wake up your body, improve circulation, and clear your mind. You can also do a few minutes of jumping jacks or a brisk walk around your living space.
  • 6:15 AM - Quick Review & Goal Setting: Spend 5-10 minutes reviewing your timetable for the day and identifying your top 1-3 priorities for your BA studies and TEFL certification. Write them down. This provides clarity and focus.
  • 6:25 AM - Personal Hygiene & Get Dressed: Complete your personal hygiene (brush teeth, wash face) and get dressed in comfortable clothes that make you feel ready to tackle the day. Avoid staying in pajamas.

Evening Routine (21:00 - 22:00 PM)

This routine focuses on relaxation, winding down, and preparing for restful sleep.

  • 21:00 PM - Digital Detox & Wind Down: Power down all electronic devices (phone, computer, gaming console). Engage in calming activities like reading a physical book, listening to soothing music, or journaling about your day. Avoid stimulating content or work-related tasks.
  • 21:30 PM - Personal Reflection & Gratitude: Spend 10-15 minutes reflecting on your day. Acknowledge your achievements, no matter how small. Practice gratitude by listing 3 things you are thankful for. This helps to shift your mindset to a positive state.
  • 21:45 PM - Prepare for Tomorrow: Lay out your clothes for the next day, pack your bag if needed, and ensure your study space is tidy. This minimizes morning stress and saves time.
  • 21:55 PM - Light Snack (Meal 6, if needed) & Hydration: If you feel hungry, have a small, easily digestible snack (e.g., a handful of nuts, a piece of fruit). Drink a glass of water. Avoid heavy meals or sugary drinks close to bedtime.
  • 22:00 PM - Prepare for Sleep: Dim the lights, ensure your bedroom is cool, dark, and quiet. Begin your sleep routine (e.g., brush teeth, wash face). Aim to be in bed by 22:00 to ensure 8 hours of sleep.

Additional Tips for Success

Weight Gain Goals

To reach your goal of 70-75kg from 56kg, focus on eating 5-6 meals per day with protein-rich foods, healthy fats, and complex carbohydrates. Consider adding protein shakes between meals.

Study Efficiency

Use the Pomodoro Technique during study blocks: 25 minutes focused study, 5-minute break. This helps maintain concentration and prevents burnout.

Flexibility

This timetable is a guide. Adjust it based on your energy levels, deadlines, and life circumstances. The key is consistency, not perfection.
