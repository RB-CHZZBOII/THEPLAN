Performance Criteria and Measuring Points

This document outlines performance criteria and measuring points for success in amateur trading, TEFL work, Unisa studies, and a weight gain journey, based on industry averages and best practices.

1. Amateur Trading

Based on the research, day trading profitability is low, with a high percentage of traders losing money. Therefore, success metrics should focus on risk management, consistent profitability (even if small), and continuous learning.

Performance Criteria:

  • Risk Management: Adherence to a defined risk-per-trade (e.g., 1-2% of capital). Maximum daily/weekly drawdown limits.
  • Profitability: Consistent positive returns over a defined period (e.g., weekly, monthly). Positive risk-reward ratio (e.g., 1:2 or 1:3).
  • Trade Management: Adherence to trading plan, entry/exit rules, and stop-loss placement.
  • Learning & Adaptation: Reviewing trades, identifying mistakes, and adapting strategies.

Measuring Points:

  • Win Rate: Percentage of profitable trades.
  • Average Win vs. Average Loss: Ratio of average profit on winning trades to average loss on losing trades.
  • Profit Factor: Gross profit divided by gross loss.
  • Maximum Drawdown: Largest peak-to-trough decline in capital.
  • Number of Trades: Consistent execution of trading opportunities.
  • Trading Journal: Detailed record of all trades, including rationale, outcomes, and lessons learned.

2. TEFL Work

Success in TEFL teaching involves student progress, classroom management, and professional development.

Performance Criteria:

  • Student Progress: Measurable improvement in student language proficiency.
  • Classroom Management: Effective and engaging learning environment.
  • Lesson Planning & Delivery: Well-structured and engaging lessons.
  • Student Engagement: Active participation and motivation of students.
  • Professional Development: Continuous learning and application of new teaching methodologies.

Measuring Points:

  • Student Assessment Scores: Improvement in quizzes, tests, and assignments.
  • Student Feedback: Surveys or informal feedback on teaching effectiveness and classroom environment.
  • Attendance & Retention: Consistent student attendance and re-enrollment rates.
  • Peer/Supervisor Observations: Formal or informal observations of teaching practice.
  • Lesson Plan Quality: Adherence to curriculum, clear objectives, and appropriate activities.

3. Unisa Studies

Success at Unisa, as an open distance learning institution, often hinges on self-discipline, time management, and consistent engagement with study materials.

Performance Criteria:

  • Academic Performance: Achieving satisfactory grades in modules.
  • Module Completion: Successful completion of registered modules within the allocated time.
  • Engagement with Materials: Consistent interaction with study guides, online resources, and assignments.
  • Time Management: Effective planning and execution of study schedule.
  • Self-Discipline: Adherence to study routine and deadlines.

Measuring Points:

  • Module Pass Rate: Percentage of modules passed each semester/year.
  • Assignment Scores: Performance on formative assessments.
  • Examination Results: Performance on summative assessments.
  • Credit Accumulation: Number of credits earned per study period.
  • Study Schedule Adherence: Tracking planned study hours versus actual study hours.

4. Weight Gain Journey

For a weight gain journey, success is not just about increasing weight but about gaining healthy mass, primarily muscle, and improving body composition.

Performance Criteria:

  • Healthy Weight Gain: Consistent increase in body weight, with a focus on muscle mass.
  • Body Composition Improvement: Decrease in body fat percentage and increase in lean muscle mass.
  • Nutritional Intake: Consistent adherence to a calorie-surplus, protein-rich diet.
  • Strength Progression: Increase in strength and lifting capacity.
  • Consistency in Training: Regular and structured resistance training.

Measuring Points:

  • Body Weight: Weekly or bi-weekly weigh-ins.
  • Body Measurements: Circumference measurements of key muscle groups (e.g., arms, chest, thighs).
  • Body Fat Percentage: Regular assessment (e.g., monthly) using methods like calipers, bioelectrical impedance, or DEXA scans.
  • Strength Metrics: Tracking progressive overload in key lifts (e.g., 1-rep max, reps at a given weight).
  • Dietary Intake Tracking: Monitoring calorie and macronutrient intake.
  • Workout Consistency: Tracking number of training sessions per week and adherence to program.
